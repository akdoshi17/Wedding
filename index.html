Our Story

Where It Started

The First Hello

It started with an ordinary afternoon that turned extraordinary — a mutual friend's gathering, a shared laugh about a terrible cup of tea, and a conversation that neither of them wanted to end. Neither Parit nor Jyoti had planned to meet the love of their life that day, but the universe had other ideas.

The First Date

Dinner for Two

Their first proper date was spent at a cozy little restaurant, talking for so long that the staff had to politely hint it was closing time. Over shared plates and stolen glances, they discovered how perfectly their worlds fit together — music tastes, terrible puns, and a shared dream of seeing the ocean together someday.

Adventures Together

Exploring the World

From spontaneous road trips to carefully planned getaways, Parit and Jyoti explored the world one adventure at a time. Each journey added a new chapter to their story — from the hills to the coast — and with every destination, they realised home was wherever they were together.

The Big Question

He Asked, She Said Yes

As the sun melted into the Indian Ocean on a golden evening, Parit got down on one knee. With a trembling voice and a heart full of love, he asked the question he'd been rehearsing for weeks. Jyoti's yes was the one word that changed everything — and made the rest of their lives the greatest adventure yet.

And Now…

The Next Chapter

Now, surrounded by the people they love most, beneath the stars on the shores of Dar es Salaam, Parit and Jyoti are ready to begin their forever. They are so grateful you're here to share this milestone with them. Let's celebrate love — loudly and joyfully.

Getting Here

Travel & Stay

By Air

Fly into Julius Nyerere International Airport (DAR), the main international gateway to Dar es Salaam. The airport is approximately 30 minutes from White Sands Resort by taxi or hotel transfer.

Major airlines flying to DAR include Ethiopian Airlines, Kenya Airways, Qatar Airways, Turkish Airlines, and Emirates.

Accommodation

We have reserved a block of rooms at White Sands Resort for wedding guests. Please mention the group code "PARIT&JYOTI2027" when booking to receive preferential rates.

We recommend booking early — Dar es Salaam is beautiful in January and rooms fill up fast!

Local Transport

The resort is easily accessible by private taxi or ride-hailing apps (Bolt and Uber are available in Dar es Salaam). We will also arrange shuttle services between the airport and the resort for guests arriving on 27 January 2027.

Details of shuttle timings will be shared closer to the wedding date.

Visa & Entry

Most nationalities require a visa to enter Tanzania. You can obtain an eVisa online before travelling via the official Tanzania eVisa portal. Processing typically takes 3–10 business days.

Apply at visa.immigration.go.tz at least 2–3 weeks before departure.

Weather in January

Dar es Salaam in late January is warm and mostly dry — perfect wedding weather! Expect temperatures of 28–34°C (82–93°F) with coastal breezes keeping things comfortable. Light, breathable fabrics are recommended.

Sunscreen is a must! The equatorial sun is strong, especially on the beach.

Explore Tanzania

Make the most of your trip! Dar es Salaam is close to Zanzibar (a 2-hour ferry or 20-min flight), Serengeti, and Mount Kilimanjaro. Consider extending your stay for a mini-safari or beach holiday.

Talk to us if you'd like help planning a Tanzania extension trip!
